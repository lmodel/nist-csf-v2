# Glossary

* [Function](function.md) - In the CSF, the highest level of organization for cybersecurity outcomes; there are six, GOVERN, IDENTIFY, PROTECT, DETECT, RESPOND and RECOVER. The schema renders a function as `CsfFunction`, with its two letters in `csf_function_id`.
* [Category](category.md) - In the CSF, a group of related cybersecurity outcomes that together make up a function, with an identifier such as GV.OC. The schema renders a category as `CsfCategory`, with its identifier in `csf_category_id`, and a withdrawn CSF 1.1 category with its status and destination.
* [Subcategory](subcategory.md) - In the CSF, a group of more specific outcomes of technical and management cybersecurity activities that make up a category, stated in a sentence with an identifier such as GV.OC-01. The schema renders a subcategory as `CsfSubcategory`, with its identifier in `csf_subcategory_id` and its statement in `description`.
* [Implementation example](implementation-example.md) - In the CSF, a concise, action-oriented, notional illustration of a way to help achieve a Core outcome, published by NIST beside the Core with an identifier such as GV.OC-01.001. The schema renders one as `CsfImplementationExample`.
* [Withdrawn element](withdrawn-element.md) - A CSF 1.1 category or subcategory that CSF 2.0 withdrew, which NIST's catalog keeps with the status `withdrawn` and, where NIST recorded it, the CSF 2.0 element it moved to or that incorporated its outcomes. The schema carries the status in `CsfElementStatus` and the destinations in the moved-to and incorporated-into attributes.

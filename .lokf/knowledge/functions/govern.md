# GOVERN (GV)

The catalog's overview of the function says, in this bundle's words, that the organization sets, communicates and monitors its strategy, expectations and policy for managing cybersecurity risk.[^nist_csf_v2.0_catalog.json] In CSF 2.0 the function holds 6 categories, 31 subcategories and 119 implementation examples, in its categories:

- `GV.OC` Organizational Context: 5 subcategories
- `GV.RM` Risk Management Strategy: 7 subcategories
- `GV.RR` Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities: 4 subcategories
- `GV.PO` Policy: 2 subcategories
- `GV.OV` Oversight: 3 subcategories
- `GV.SC` Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management: 10 subcategories

GOVERN is new in CSF 2.0, and the catalog keeps no withdrawn element under it. Several withdrawn CSF 1.1 categories of IDENTIFY were incorporated into its categories: ID.BE into GV.OC, ID.GV into the function itself, ID.RM into GV.RM and ID.SC into GV.SC.

# Explanations

* [Scope, influences and mappings](scope-and-influences.md) - What the schema renders and what it leaves out: the CSF 2.0 of CSWP 29 and nothing beyond it, with lmodel/slsa as its one influence on layout. It maps its classes to lmodel/oscal's, as NIST's catalog renders them, keeps the withdrawn CSF 1.1 elements as the catalog states them, and models neither the Informative References, the Profiles and Tiers, the risk-party marking nor the community profiles.
* [Element names and the shared namespace](element-names.md) - How the schema names its elements so that its adapt copy can sit beside LOKF's schema and the slsa, github-actions, oscal, spdx and nist-sp-800-218 copies: classes with the `Csf` prefix, NIST's identifiers kept as written, the identifier keys prefixed with `csf_`, every other key qualified with the CSF's own nouns, `title` and `description` carrying LOKF's IRIs, and no other key shared, which `tests/test_schema_rules.py` checks.
